01

A small setup, then you're ready

Bring a lecture. Bring your key.

First

Connect an AI provider

An API key lets DeLectured send requests using your provider account. Open Settings, choose a transcription provider for audio and a notes provider for study materials.

Use the provider's Get key link, create a key on its website, then paste it into the matching field here. One key is enough when both tasks use the same provider. Keep the preset model IDs unless you need a different supported model.

Then

Record or upload

Save your settings, open Capture and choose English, Hindi or Hinglish. Allow microphone access to record, or upload an existing audio file.

Uploads support browser-decodable MP3, M4A, WAV and MP4, up to 500 MiB and two hours. Files are compressed locally into five-minute parts. Keep the tab open; interrupted imports can continue with the original file. Provider usage limits still apply. Recording has its own live timer and waveform.

Finally

Build your understanding

Stop and save your lecture. Once transcription and analysis finish, explore the study page or return later through Library.

Follow the topic timeline, check source quotes, ask a question or test yourself with recall cards. Export Markdown, Anki CSV or a printable PDF.

03

Know where your work lives

Before you press record

Where do my recordings, notes and keys go?

Your library and pending audio use this browser's storage. Audio goes directly to your selected transcription provider; transcript passages go to your notes provider for analysis and questions. Keys are stored in browser settings and sent to the configured provider. DeLectured has no application backend.

Successfully transcribed audio is removed when its text is saved. Browser data can be cleared or evicted: use Library's backup export before clearing it or changing devices.

What if the connection fails or storage is full?

Saved audio can wait in the queue for recovery. If storage is full, unsaved audio stays in RAM: export it or free space and retry before reloading. RAM audio and unfinished recording buffers do not survive a reload.

When provider attempts are exhausted, retained audio stays available for your decision. Read the reported error, check your key or account limits, then retry or export.

Do I need an account or subscription?

DeLectured has no application login or subscription. You need an account and API key with your chosen AI provider. Any free allowance, payment requirement and usage limits are set by that provider; the app does not promise free inference.

Can I trust every note and answer?

No. Transcription can miss words, and AI explanations can be wrong. Review the transcript and quoted evidence, especially for formulas, names and exam preparation. Connections and recall cards depend on what could be extracted from the lecture.
