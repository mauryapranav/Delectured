A short field guide

Make room for understanding.

Keys and providers

Set your transcription and notes provider in Keys. Audio and transcript text are sent directly to those providers. Provider charges and quotas are separate from this static app.

Capture and recovery

Recording saves completed audio parts in browser storage and transcribes when connected. Successful transcription removes its audio. Interruptions can lose the unfinished part. Uploads are limited to 500 MiB and two hours.

If storage is full, export RAM-only audio or make space and retry before reloading. RAM-only audio does not survive reload. Pending stored audio can be resumed or discarded in recovery.

Library and study materials

View, search, rename and delete saved lectures in Library. Export JSON backups regularly; browser storage is not a permanent guarantee. Choose explicitly whether an import may replace existing IDs.

Reports export to Markdown and Anki CSV, or selectable text through Print / Save as PDF. In Anki choose comma-separated fields, map the two columns to front/back, and disable HTML interpretation. Formula-like CSV cells have a protective apostrophe.

Regenerate notes uses current notes settings. Q&A searches selected transcript passages; inspect quoted evidence because retrieval can miss relevant content and AI interpretation can be incorrect.

After finishing or exporting pending work, close every app tab and reopen to activate an available update. A hosting-origin change requires exporting and importing your Library.
